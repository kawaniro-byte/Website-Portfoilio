About Me

I'm Jeru, a second-year Information Technology student at the University of Cabuyao (PnC). I'm most interested in the front end — the part of a product people actually touch — and I like the discipline of getting spacing, color, and hierarchy right, not just getting a page to "work."

Outside of class, I study on my own, mentor incoming freshmen on Git basics, and I'm slowly building a habit of shipping one small project every month.
